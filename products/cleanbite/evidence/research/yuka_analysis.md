# Competitor Teardown: Yuka (yuka.io)

## 📊 Overview
* **Name:** Yuka
* **URL:** `https://yuka.io/en/`
* **Core Value Prop:** Deciphers product labels and analyzes the health impact of food products and cosmetics with 0-100 scores.
* **Key Positioning:** 100% independent project, no brand influence, no ads, responsible financing via premium subscriptions.

## 🔑 Strengths to Adopt
1. **Instant Clarity:** Visual 0-100 score + 4 color levels (Green, Light Green, Orange, Red).
2. **Immediate Healthier Alternatives:** Showing smart swaps right below a poor product reduces purchase regret.
3. **Additive Risk Breakdown:** Separating nutritional balance from hazardous chemical additives.

## 🚀 CleanBite's Unfair Advantages & Modern Upgrades
1. **Interactive Web Scanner & Browser Widget:** Yuka is mobile-only; CleanBite offers instant camera scanning on both mobile & desktop plus browser grocery checkout integration (Instacart/Amazon Fresh).
2. **Personalized Allergen & Bio-Filter:** Custom filters for Seed Oils, Sugar Alcohols, Microplastics, and Endocrine Disruptors.
3. **Playful & Tactile UX:** Modern interactive micro-animations, tactile sound/haptic toggles, and live simulated scan demo right on the homepage.
