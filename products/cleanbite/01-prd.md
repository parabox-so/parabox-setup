# 1. Product Requirements Document (PRD): CleanBite

> **Product Name:** CleanBite  
> **Tagline:** Scan your food. Expose hidden junk. Eat with total confidence.  
> **Inspired by:** Yuka (yuka.io)

---

## 🎯 1. Problem Statement
Grocery shelves are packed with ultra-processed foods disguised as "healthy". Ingredient labels are written in confusing scientific jargon, obscuring harmful additives, artificial dyes (Red 40, Yellow 5), endocrine disruptors, and hidden added sugars. Consumers spend unnecessary time decoding microscopic fine print and still end up purchasing products that compromise their long-term metabolic and gut health.

---

## 👥 2. Target User
* **Health-Conscious Shoppers & Parents:** Looking to protect their families from toxic preservatives and ultra-processed food additives.
* **Special Diet Individuals:** People avoiding seed oils, microplastics, gluten, or artificial sweeteners.
* **Fitness Enthusiasts:** Tracking real nutritional quality (protein-to-calorie ratio, real whole ingredients) rather than misleading marketing claims.

---

## 🚀 3. Core Features

### 1. 📷 Real-Time Label & Barcode Scanner
* Instantly decodes product barcodes and OCR nutrition labels in <200ms.
* Produces an unambiguous **0 to 100 Health Score** with color-coded clarity:
  * 🟢 **75–100 (Excellent)**
  * 🟡 **50–74 (Good)**
  * 🟠 **25–49 (Poor)**
  * 🔴 **0–24 (Bad)**

### 2. 🔍 Additive & Toxin Radar
* Flags harmful additives, artificial dyes, microplastics, endocrine disruptors, and ultra-processing markers.
* Clearly explains *why* an additive is flagged with cited independent scientific research (no industry bias).

### 3. 🔄 Instant Clean Swaps Engine
* When a product scores Poor or Bad, CleanBite instantly recommends 3 cleaner, healthier alternatives readily available at mainstream grocery stores.
* Displays side-by-side comparison of ingredients and score improvements (e.g. *+68 points healthier*).

---

## 🛡️ 4. 100% Independent Trust Promise
* **0 Brand Sponsorships:** Brands cannot pay to improve scores or buy ads.
* **100% Unbiased Algorithm:** Evaluates only nutritional quality (60%), additive risk (30%), and organic status (10%).

---

## 💳 5. Pricing Tiers
* **Free Starter ($0):** 50 scans / month, basic health score, top additive alerts.
* **Pro Foodie ($4.99/mo or $39/yr):** Unlimited scans, offline scanning mode, custom dietary filters (Seed Oil Free, Gluten Free, Keto, Vegan), and personalized ingredient blacklist.
* **Family Kitchen ($9.99/mo):** 5 user seats, shared grocery pantry analysis, smart weekly shopping lists.
